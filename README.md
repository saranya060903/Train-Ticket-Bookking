🚆 Online Train Ticket Booking System

🎟️ A Smart and User-Friendly Train Reservation Platform

📌 About the Project

The Online Train Ticket Booking System is a web-based application designed to simplify train reservations. It includes separate Admin and Customer Portals for efficient train management and ticket booking.

Administrators can log in to the admin panel, add new trains, and manage train details. Newly added trains are reflected in the customer portal, allowing customers to view available trains and book tickets conveniently.

🔄 How It Works

🔐 The admin logs in to the admin portal.
🚄 The admin adds a new train and enters its details.
🗄️ The train information is saved to the database.
🔄 The customer portal retrieves the updated train information.
👤 Customers can search for the newly added train and book tickets.

🎯 Project Objective

To develop an efficient train ticket booking platform that connects train administration and customer reservations through a centralized system.
